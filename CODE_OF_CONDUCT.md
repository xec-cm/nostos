# Code of Conduct

recoverome welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds,
identities, and levels of experience. Everyone participating in project
discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces is expected to
treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence constructively.
- Give and receive feedback with care.
- Respect different experiences, viewpoints, and communication needs.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help resolve misunderstandings.
- Respect privacy and use only data appropriate to share publicly.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publication of private information without permission are not
acceptable. Disagreement about scientific methods is welcome when it remains
focused on evidence and does not become a personal attack.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Francesc Catala-Moll, at
<fcatala@irsicaixa.es>. Include the information needed to understand the incident;
do not post a confidential report in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports with care and limit disclosure to what is
needed to respond. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal
of inappropriate content, or restriction of participation. The response should
be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Retaliation against someone
for raising a concern is not acceptable.

This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct when someone is explicitly
representing the project elsewhere.
